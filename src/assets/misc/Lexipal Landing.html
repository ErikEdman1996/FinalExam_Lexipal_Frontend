The reading session

A complete loop, in one quiet screen.

Open a book, start the timer, and Lexipal stays out of your way until you tap a word. Everything you save flows into your library — automatically.

01 / Start

Set a timer

Pick 15, 25, or your own. Lexipal keeps the screen awake so you don't have to fight your phone.

02 / Read

Read in peace

One column of warm type. No notifications, no shelves, no streak nags. Just the page.

03 / Look up

Tap a word

Definitions surface in place, in the same paper tone. Save it to your Codex with one tap.

04 / Reflect

Note as you go

Quotes, thoughts, page-bound notes. They live with the book, not in some folder you'll never reopen.

05 / Finish

Wrap with a recap

Time read, words saved, pages turned. A small XP nudge — never the point.

Stay awake, stay focused

The mobile reader keeps the screen on, dims the rest of your phone, and silences nothing — your phone, your rules.

Word lookup, in the page

Tap once. Definition, pronunciation, and the sentence you found it in — all kept together.

Notes that stay with the book

Reflections live next to the chapter that sparked them. Searchable, exportable, never orphaned.

Bookmarks that remember

Pick up exactly where you left off — even mid-paragraph, mid-sentence, mid-thought.

A small reading room, just for the people you trust.

Book Circles are private spaces for two to twenty readers. Read the same book on your own time — see each other's notes, words, and reflections when you finish a chapter.

Invite-only by default

No discoverable feed. Share a link with friends, classmates, or your study group — that's the whole circle.

Spoiler-aware sharing

Notes only unlock for circle members once they've read the same chapter. No accidental reveals.

Optional meetings

Pin a meeting date for the chapter you're discussing. No pressure — most circles never schedule one.
